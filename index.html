How to play
  • Click empty land to expand into it. Click a neighbour's land to attack them.
  • The Send slider sets how many of your troops each click commits.
  • Troops grow fastest when you are well below your cap, so keep spending them.
  • Each player has a capital, marked with a star. Losing yours costs half your troops.
  • Cut off enemy land from its capital, or surround empty land, and it becomes yours.
